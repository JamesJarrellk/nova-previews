Questions

The ones we get first.

Do I need to be home?

No. Most customers leave a key, a code, or a garage pin. Tell us how you'd like us in and we note it on your file.

Do you bring supplies?

Yes, everything, including vacuums. If you prefer a specific product for a surface, leave it out and tell us.

Is it the same team every time?

That's the goal, and it's the norm for recurring customers. If someone's out, the team lead knows your house.

How is the price set?

By the size of the home, the plan, and how often we come. We text you the number before anything is booked. Recurring visits cost less per visit than a one-time clean.

What if something gets missed?

Tell us within 24 hours and we come back and re-clean that area at no additional cost. That's The Maids 24-hour satisfaction guarantee, and we keep it locally.

Can I reschedule?

Yes. Text or call before the day prior and we'll move it. Same-day changes depend on the route.

Pets and kids?

Fine with both. We use products chosen for homes with children and pets, and we'll work around a nervous dog.

Are you the real The Maids?

Yes. We're the independently owned and operated Murfreesboro franchise of The Maids, run from Thompson Lane. Same 22-step process and guarantee as the national brand, with a local team and a local phone number. You can also book through maids.com.
