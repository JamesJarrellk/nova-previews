Questions

The ones we get first.

What is included in a standard home cleaning?

The rooms in your chosen plan, cleaned with the 22-Step Cleaning Process: disinfecting high-touch areas, hard-surface floors, and the details in every room.

How long does a typical cleaning take?

It depends on the size and condition of the home and the plan. Our teams work together, so most homes are done in a fraction of the time one cleaner would take.

Do I need to be home?

No. Most customers leave a key, a code, or a garage pin. Tell us how you'd like us in and we note it on your file.

Do you bring supplies?

Yes. Professional tools and products, commercial-grade vacuums with HEPA filtration, EPA-approved disinfectants, and non-toxic cleaning supplies safe for kids and pets.

Is it the same team every time?

That's the goal, and it's the norm for recurring customers. If someone's out, the team lead knows your house.

How is the price set?

By the size of the home, the plan, and how often we come. We text you the number before anything is booked. Recurring visits cost less per visit than a one-time clean.

What if something gets missed?

If you are not satisfied with any area we cleaned, call within 24 hours and the team will come back to re-clean that area at no additional cost. That's The Maids 24-hour 100% satisfaction guarantee, and we keep it locally.

Can I reschedule?

Yes. Text or call before the day prior and we'll move it. Same-day changes depend on the route.

Pets and kids?

Fine with both. Non-toxic products, HEPA-filter vacuums for pet hair and dander, and we'll work around a nervous dog.

Are you the real The Maids?

Yes. We're the independently owned and operated Murfreesboro franchise of The Maids, run from Thompson Lane. Same 22-step process and guarantee as the national brand, with a local team and a local phone number. You can also book through maids.com.
