Questions

The ones we get first.

How much does a fence cost?

It depends on the type, the height, the footage, the number of gates, and the ground. Chain link is the least per foot, vinyl the most. The estimate is free and in writing, so you'll have a real number before anything gets dug.

Do I need a permit?

Inside Dickson city limits, sometimes. In the county, usually not for a residential fence. We'll tell you what applies to your address and handle the paperwork if there is any.

What about my property line?

We build to the line you show us, and we'll flag it if something looks off. If you're not sure where the line is, a survey before we dig is cheaper than moving a fence after.

Will you call before you dig?

Yes. Tennessee 811 gets called before every job so the gas, water, and power lines get marked.

How long does it take?

Most residential jobs are one to three days once materials are on site. Posts go in first, concrete cures, then the fence goes up. Weather can move things a day.

Can you fix my existing fence instead of replacing it?

Often, yes. Leaning posts, bent rails, sagging gates, and storm damage are everyday repairs. If the posts are rotted through, replacing is the honest answer, and we'll say so.

Do you do farm fence and large properties?

Yes. Woven wire, board fence, field fence, and farm gates. Priced by the run. Send the acreage and a rough idea of the layout.

Do you build gates?

Walk gates, double drive gates, and farm gates, built to match the fence and hung so they still swing true next year. Gate repairs and re-hangs too.
